Arsenal Daily Bot

The day’s Arsenal FC news in one short video, made automatically every morning.

Where to watch

YouTube Shorts@nebexscreen TikTok@nebexscreen

How it works

  1. Gather the news. Collects the latest Arsenal headlines from public news sources, plus public fan discussion from Reddit’s r/Gunners.
  2. Write the script. An AI model (Anthropic’s Claude) turns the headlines into a short, five-slide summary.
  3. Record the voiceover. The script is read by a synthetic voice.
  4. Build the video. Each slide gets a background photo that’s free to reuse (from Wikimedia Commons or Pexels), and every photographer and licence is credited in the video description.
  5. Publish. The finished vertical video is posted to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Our principles

  • Our own accounts only. The bot only connects to channels we own. It has no public sign-ups and never posts on anyone else’s behalf.
  • No viewer data. We don’t collect personal data from viewers. These pages use no cookies or tracking.
  • Real quotes only. Fan comments are quoted word for word from public Reddit posts. If there isn’t a real quote, the video doesn’t show one.
  • Credit where it’s due. Only freely licensed photos are used, and each one is credited.
