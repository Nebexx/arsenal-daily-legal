Privacy Policy

Arsenal Daily Bot · Effective September 4, 2026

Arsenal Daily Bot (“the Service”) is an automated tool operated by Nebiyou Girma (“we”, “us”). Once a day it creates a short Arsenal FC news video and posts it to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the Service handles. In short: it doesn’t collect personal data from viewers or the public, and it only connects to accounts we own.

1. Who the Service is for

The Service is not a public app. Nobody can sign up for it, log in to it or connect their own account to it. The only people who authorise it are its operators, for their own channels.

We don’t collect data about people who watch our videos. Views, likes and comments are handled by TikTok and YouTube under their own privacy policies. These pages use no cookies, analytics or tracking.

3. Services that process data for us

  • Anthropic (Claude API) receives the day’s public headlines and Reddit text to write the video script.
  • Microsoft Edge text-to-speech receives the script text to create the voiceover.
  • Wikimedia Commons and Pexels receive photo search terms, such as a player’s name, to find freely licensed background photos.
  • GitHub runs the daily job and stores encrypted secrets and run logs.
  • TikTok and YouTube receive the finished video, title, description and photo credits when we publish.

None of these services receive personal data about viewers. We don’t sell, rent or share data for advertising.

4. How long we keep data

Platform tokens are kept until we revoke them or stop using the Service. Run logs keep only the last 30 runs. Scraped headlines and posts are overwritten on each run.

5. Revoking access and deleting data

We can revoke the Service’s access at any time: on TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and on Google under Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless, and we then delete them from our secrets.

If you’ve been quoted in a video and want it removed, email nebexg@yahoo.com with a link and we’ll review and remove it where appropriate.

6. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Security

Credentials are stored only as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and in gitignored files on the operator’s computer. They are never committed to source control or shown in logs.

8. Changes to this policy

We’ll update this page if the Service starts handling data differently. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
